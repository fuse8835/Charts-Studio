Chart Studio

All charts in the Fraser Institute climate-mortality series, in the order they were built. Open a chart to preview, scrub, and annotate it, or render its final ProRes 4444 alpha .mov directly from here.

Rendering runs this same local server through a headless Chrome capture + ffmpeg encode — it can take a minute or two per chart, and only one render can run at a time. Keep this terminal window open while a render is in progress. New chart files just need an entry in _render/charts.json and a <script src="render-client.js" data-chart-id="..."> tag to show up here with a working render button.
