Charts Explainers

Explainers

Pieces that fall outside the numbered chart series' shared axis+line/bar template -- map/annotation charts and standalone motion graphics, each its own argument or mechanism rather than a data chart.

Everything here is a real entry in _render/charts.json -- same render pipeline as the main catalogue, just excluded from index.html's list because it doesn't fit the shared axis+line/bar template. A standalone motion graphic with no natural chart id can still be added as a plain static card linking straight to its page; anything that gets its own id (like 5C) shows up here automatically through the dynamic list below instead.
