The 8A fill recipe

Three stops blend colour and transparency together. Keep the top vibrant; deepen the hue as it approaches the base.

PositionToneOpacity
0%Bright category colour88%

During an area reveal, the gradient follows the highest point currently visible—not the final peak. Decorative waves never represent extra data.

COLOUR REFERENCE

The 8A palette

Bright → middle → dark

Energy-source colours keep their 8A meanings. For other subjects, mint is the primary series and gold orange is the emphasis colour. Use direct labels, markers or dash patterns as well as colour. Forest and Ocean work best in larger filled regions; avoid them for tiny points against navy.

CONSISTENCY FOR FUTURE CHARTS

Keep the data in charge.

Canvas & typography

Use a 1200 × 750 design canvas; export at 1920 × 1200. Keep content about 6% in from the edges. Rajdhani 700 for titles, 600 for labels, 500 for axes. Put the source small at bottom right.

Lines & texture

At 1200 × 750: filled boundaries 1.5–2 px; standalone data lines 3–4 px; grid 1 px at 12–18% opacity. Use 24–36 organic waves at 10–15% opacity within generous filled regions. Reduce texture on small marks.

Meaning & readability

Bars start at zero. Bubble size encodes area. Keep stacked category order stable. Label units and log scales explicitly. Use colour plus labels or shapes. Heatmaps use an honest intensity scale rather than decorative gradients.

Motion & delivery

Establish axes, reveal data, then add interpretation. Keep a one-second still hold between narration stages. Circle callouts draw their outline before the fill and words. Hide navigation and controls in exports.
